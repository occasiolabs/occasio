Your agent has your shell, your files, your secrets.

You handed an AI agent the keys. Do you actually know what it did with them?

▟ destructive

It runs real commands

One bad curl | sh, one rm on the wrong path. In one 2025 incident an agent executed 5,000+ shell commands across a single run.

▌ exfiltration

It reads your secrets

An agent (or a poisoned MCP server) reads .env and sends it to the cloud as ordinary context. You never see it leave.

▖ no record

You can't prove anything

When someone asks "did the agent touch prod / read that file?", a normal log is no answer — it can be edited after the fact.

Record. Enforce. Prove.

Three things, one local-first tool — no cloud, no telemetry, ever.

Record

Black-box recorder

Every tool call — Read, Edit, Bash, MCP — captured locally as it happens. The flight recorder for your agent.

Enforce

Policy at the boundary

Block dangerous actions before they run. Deny-lists, path rules, and more — and the block is recorded too.

Prove

Tamper-evident attestation

Hash-chained from a GENESIS sentinel, Sigstore-signed. A third party verifies the whole history — without trusting you.

It doesn't just watch — it stops the leak.

Most tools observe what the agent sent. Occasio strips denied files and secrets out of the request body before it leaves your machine.

Egress enforcement

Denied content never ships

When the agent pre-bakes a denied file or a secret into the outbound request, Occasio resolves the real path (symlinks too), removes the content, and forwards the rest. The model never receives it.

Honest coverage

It tells you what it couldn't intercept

Every tool call gets a coverage code. No silent gaps, no inflated claims — you see exactly what was enforced and what fell through.

Verify in 60 seconds

Hand someone the bundle. They confirm it's genuine offline — no account, no network to us.
